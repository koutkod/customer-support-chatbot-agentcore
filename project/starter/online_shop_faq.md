# Aurora & Co. Online Shop — Frequently Asked Questions

*Aurora & Co. is a fictional online shop used for this project. Every policy
below is invented for the purposes of the chatbot's FAQ-answering behavior.*

## Orders

**How do I check the status of my order?**
Log in to your account and go to Order History. Each order shows one of the
following statuses: Processing, Packed, Shipped, Out for Delivery, or
Delivered. You'll also receive an email every time the status changes.

**Can I change or cancel an order after placing it?**
You can change or cancel an order only while it is still in "Processing"
status, which usually lasts up to 1 hour after checkout. Go to Order History
and select "Cancel Order" or "Edit Order." Once an order moves to "Packed,"
it can no longer be changed or canceled — you'll need to wait for delivery
and start a return instead.

**Why was my order canceled automatically?**
Orders are automatically canceled if payment authorization fails twice, if
an item goes out of stock before packing, or if the order isn't confirmed
within 24 hours for a saved-cart checkout. You will not be charged for an
automatically canceled order, and any temporary authorization hold is
released within 3–5 business days.

**Can I combine two separate orders into one shipment?**
No. Each order placed is packed and shipped independently, even if placed
minutes apart. This is so that in-stock items aren't held up waiting for
items in a second order.

## Shipping

**What shipping options are available?**
- Standard Shipping: 5–7 business days, free on orders over $50 (otherwise $4.99)
- Express Shipping: 2–3 business days, $12.99
- Next-Day Shipping: available for orders placed before 12pm local time on
  eligible items, $24.99

**Do you ship internationally?**
We currently ship to the United States, Canada, and the United Kingdom.
International orders may be subject to customs fees and import duties,
which are the customer's responsibility and are not included in the
checkout price.

**How do I track my shipment?**
Once an order ships, you'll receive a tracking link by email and can also
find it under Order History → View Tracking. Tracking updates can take up
to 24 hours to appear after the shipping label is created.

**My tracking says "Delivered" but I don't have my package. What do I do?**
Check with neighbors and any building mailroom or leasing office first,
since carriers sometimes mark packages delivered up to 24 hours before they
physically arrive. If it hasn't turned up after 48 hours, contact our
support line so we can file a claim with the carrier.

## Returns

**What is your return policy?**
Most items can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund,
provided they are unused, unworn, and in their original packaging with
tags attached. Final-sale items (marked as such on the product page) and
gift cards are not eligible for return.

**How do I start a return?**
Go to Order History, select the order, and click "Start a Return." Choose a
reason, print the prepaid return label, and drop the package off at any
carrier location. Return shipping is free for defective or incorrect items;
a $5.99 return shipping fee is deducted from the refund for all other
reasons.

**How long does a refund take once you receive my return?**
Refunds are issued within 5 business days of the returned item arriving at
our warehouse. It can take an additional 3–10 business days for the refund
to appear on your original payment method, depending on your bank or card
issuer.

**Can I exchange an item instead of returning it?**
We don't process direct exchanges. Return the original item for a refund
and place a new order for the item or size you want — this is faster than
waiting for an exchange to process, and ensures the replacement item is
reserved for you right away.

## Payments

**What payment methods do you accept?**
We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, PayPal, and Aurora
& Co. gift cards. We do not currently accept cryptocurrency or buy-now-pay-later
services.

**When is my card charged?**
Your card is authorized at checkout but only charged once your order enters
"Packed" status. If part of your order ships separately, each shipment is
charged individually for just the items it contains.

**Why was my payment declined?**
The most common causes are an incorrect billing address (it must match the
address on file with your card issuer), insufficient funds, or a bank
fraud hold on a first-time purchase from a new merchant. Contact your card
issuer to confirm before trying again.

**Is my payment information stored securely?**
We never store your full card number. Payments are processed through a
PCI-DSS compliant payment processor, and only a tokenized reference is kept
on file if you choose to save a card for future orders.

## Products & Account

**How do I find my size?**
Each product page has a "Size Guide" link with measurements in inches and
centimeters. If you're between sizes, our general recommendation is
printed on the size guide for that specific product category.

**How do I reset my password?**
Click "Forgot password?" on the sign-in page and enter the email address on
your account. You'll receive a reset link that expires after 60 minutes.

**How do I update my email or shipping address?**
Go to Account Settings to update your email, and to Address Book to add,
edit, or remove shipping addresses. Changing your address book does not
affect orders that have already been placed.

**Do you offer a loyalty or rewards program?**
Yes — Aurora Circle is free to join and earns 1 point per $1 spent. 100
points equals a $5 reward. Enroll from your Account Settings page; points
apply automatically to future orders and never expire as long as your
account remains active.
